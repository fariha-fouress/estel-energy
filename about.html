About Estel Energy

Built for the way commodity trade should work.

A privately held import-export firm trading agricultural products, polymers, cosmetics and metals — connecting producers and buyers across continents with the discipline of a trading desk and the precision of a logistics operator.

Who we are

From producer to buyer, we close the loop.

Estel Energy is a privately held import-export firm. We specialize in end-to-end commodity trading backed by robust logistics, strict quality control, and efficient supply chain management. Our global presence ensures seamless procurement and delivery across continents.

We trade four core categories — agricultural products, polymers, cosmetics, and metals — sourcing from vetted suppliers and delivering to manufacturers, distributors, and industrial buyers worldwide. Every cargo is paper-clean, every counterparty pre-screened, every shipment tracked.

We are committed to ensuring timely access to products, strengthening supply chains through responsive service, and building enduring partnerships based on mutual growth. We don't disappear after the contract is signed — we stay involved through clearance, settlement, and the next order.

What we stand for

Three principles, applied to every cargo.

The same standards apply whether the order is one container or a hundred. That consistency is what our partners come back for.

01

Quality without compromise

Every shipment is inspected against agreed specifications before it leaves origin. If it doesn't meet the standard, it doesn't ship. No exceptions, no fine print.

02

Timely, every time

We hit 98% on-time delivery because we plan for the things that go wrong, not just the things that go right. Real-time cargo tracking and direct carrier relationships keep cargo moving.

03

Partnerships, not transactions

We treat counterparties as long-term partners, not single deals. Our stakeholders bring decades of relationships built that way — and Estel Energy is built to continue them.
