What we trade

Multiple product verticals. One disciplined desk.

From cropland to refinery, raw metal to retail-ready cosmetics — we source, ship, and settle across various categories, each with dedicated specialists and audited supply chains.

Vertical 01

Agro Products

Agricultural commodities sourced from vetted growers and cooperatives across major producing regions. Rice, grain, oilseeds, sugar, pulses, animal feed and processed foods — moved in bulk under quality-controlled, traceable supply chains.

  • Rice — long-grain, short-grain and parboiled varieties
  • Bulk grain, oilseeds, sugar, pulses
  • Animal feed and feed ingredients
  • Processed and packaged food items
  • Origin certificates and phytosanitary documentation
Vertical 02

Polymers

Industrial-grade polymers and resins for converters, manufacturers and packaging producers. Sourced directly from major petrochemical producers including Siam Cement Group, IRPC Public Co. Ltd, Formosa Plastics Corporation and GPPC.

  • PE, PP, PVC, PET, PS and engineering resins
  • Spot and term contracts, FOB or CIF
  • Container and bulk vessel loads
Vertical 03

Cosmetics

Finished cosmetic goods and raw cosmetic ingredients for personal care brands and contract manufacturers — surfactants, emulsifiers, fragrances and packaged formulations from audited suppliers.

Vertical 04

Metals

Industrial metals for manufacturers, fabricators and infrastructure projects. We handle ferrous and non-ferrous categories with mill certificates, third-party inspection, and traceable origin documentation.

  • Steel — hot-rolled, cold-rolled, galvanized coil
  • Aluminum ingots, billets and extrusions
  • Copper cathode and scrap categories
